## Safety and evidence

- Draft only: never send, click Submit, or submit an application.
- Never fabricate facts, metrics, credentials, projects, employers, dates,
  salary, work authorization, or interview stories. Ask when evidence is absent.
- Run `npm run cv:verify-facts` before presenting a generated CV as complete.
- Prefer a direct employer posting over a mirror or aggregator.

## CV and application writing

- Default to one A4 page. Preserve at least 9.5pt body text and 0.45in margins.
- Follow the job description's language unless the candidate asks otherwise.
- Make experience bullets outcome first: result, evidence, method. Use XYZ
  metrics only when a source proves them; otherwise use a truthful reduced form.
- Keep cover letters concise: normally 280-330 words and no more than 3 bullets.
  Follow the employer posting's structure.
- Prefer email for first contact when a verified address exists. Mark contact
  evidence as `VERIFIED`, `PUBLIC`, `INFERRED`, or `UNKNOWN`.

## Evaluation and workflow

- Treat adjacent tools and skills as bridgeable unless the JD makes one a real
  hard requirement.
- Use the standard model tier for triage and a higher tier only for roles that
  proceed to full evaluation.
- Reuse a local JD from `jds/` or the JD index before fetching it again.
- Keep batch work resumable, persist progress, and fail closed on bad input.

## Output preferences

- Lead with the verdict and next action.
- Be concrete and concise. Avoid generic AI filler and em dashes.
- Show unknowns explicitly instead of smoothing over them.
