## Default voice

- Clear, direct, calm, and specific.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete verbs.
- State evidence before claims.
- Keep uncertainty visible: use `unknown` or ask a question.

## Avoid

- Invented confidence, metrics, or personal details.
- Buzzword piles and generic phrases such as "passionate professional",
  "results-driven", "leveraged", "synergy", or "game-changing".
- Em dashes, inflated adjectives, and unnecessary summaries.
